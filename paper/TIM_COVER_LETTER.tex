Dear Editor-in-Chief,

Please consider our manuscript, ``Task-Aware Validity and Uncertainty of
Image-Fidelity Metrics for Visible-Infrared Image Fusion,'' for publication as a
Regular Paper.

Image-fidelity scores are often used as indirect measurements when selecting a
visible-infrared fusion rule. The central problem is not whether one fusion image
looks better in isolation, but whether a fidelity score preserves the ordering of
downstream task performance under a defined measurement context. Our study develops
a task-aware validity and uncertainty audit for this indirect measurement. It holds
the fused inputs fixed while varying the reference detector, source convention,
fidelity-index construction, training seed, test sample and spatial-alignment stratum.

Using fourteen fused inputs, 26 detection checkpoints, two pedestrian-detection
benchmarks and one segmentation benchmark, we show that the resulting ranking is not
a stable property of the fused images alone. Under three independently tested
visible-trained detectors, all 42 evaluator-input contrasts are negative, with mAP50
deficits from 0.046 to 0.246. A modality-agnostic detector reverses the sign for four
inputs, and two constructions of the same VIF family place the same method first and
last. The study separates these effects from sampling and training uncertainty rather
than combining unlike components into a single score.

We believe the work fits IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement because
it addresses measurement validity, repeatability, sensitivity and reporting for an
image-derived proxy rather than presenting another image-fusion architecture. It
provides an explicit measurand, reference measurement and uncertainty audit, and it
releases the per-image scores, fused images, split manifests and analysis scripts so
that every interval and ranking diagnostic can be recomputed. The conclusions are
bounded to the measured pedestrian-detection populations and explicitly acknowledge
that source alignment remains a material factor.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has been
approved by all authors. The authors declare no competing interests. Data and code
availability are stated in the manuscript.
