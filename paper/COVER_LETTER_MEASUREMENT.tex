\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

We submit the manuscript ``Evaluator-Dependent Validity of Fidelity Metrics for
Visible-Infrared Image Fusion in Pedestrian Detection'' for consideration as a
Research Paper in \emph{Measurement}.

The manuscript treats image-fidelity scores as measurement instruments rather than
assuming that a higher score implies a better downstream input. It holds the fused
images fixed while changing the detector, source convention and test partition. In the
primary independent-test experiment, all 42 comparisons across three visible-trained
evaluators place every fused input below the unfused visible source, with mean mAP50
deficits from 0.046 to 0.246. The 14 inputs under each single-run YOLO26n and RT-DETR-l
evaluator remain below the source after Benjamini-Hochberg correction. A modality-agnostic
evaluator reverses the sign for several inputs, showing that the ranking measures the
evaluation context as well as the fused image.

We believe this fits \emph{Measurement} because it reports a measurement-validity study
and a reproducible evaluation protocol: a fixed 1,350/150/300 split, test-independent
checkpoint selection, paired image resampling, multiplicity correction, explicit
evaluator definitions and a reproducible release of per-image scores. The study also
reports where the original validation protocol is optimistic, which is directly
relevant to readers who use proxy scores to select sensing or image-processing
pipelines.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under
consideration by another journal. All authors have approved the submission and declare
no competing interests. The analysis package, split manifest and trained clean
checkpoints are prepared for release with the article.

\closing{Sincerely,}
